
# **ASSIGNMENT – 1**

## **Practice Problems**

### **Level–1 (Basic)**

1. A 4 m long cantilever is subjected to a point load of 12 kN at free end.  
   Tasks: Draw SF & BM diagrams and find maximum BM.

2. A 5 m long SSB carries UDL of 8 kN/m over entire span.  
   Tasks: Find reactions and draw SF & BM diagrams.

3. A 3 m cantilever carries a moment of 15 kNm at free end.  
   Task: Draw SF & BM diagrams.

---

### **Level–2 (Intermediate)**

4. A 6 m SSB carries:
   - Point load 10 kN at 2 m from A
   - Point load 20 kN at 4 m from A  
   Tasks: Draw SF & BM diagrams and find maximum BM.

5. A 7 m SSB carries UDL of 5 kN/m over 4 m from left.  
   Tasks: Draw SF & BM diagrams and find BM under end of UDL.

6. A 5 m cantilever carries UDL of 2 kN/m over 3 m from free end.  
   Task: Draw SF & BM diagrams and locate maximum moment.

---

### **Level–3 (Advanced)**

7. An 8 m SSB carries:
   - UDL 6 kN/m full span
   - Clockwise moment 25 kNm at 3 m from A  
   Tasks: Draw SF & BM diagrams and find maximum BM.

8. A 6 m overhanging beam with supports at 1 m and 5 m from left carries:
   - 20 kN point load on left overhang
   - UDL 4 kN/m between supports  
   Tasks: Draw SF & BM diagrams and comment on POC.

9. A 10 m SSB carries triangular UVL (0 at A → 8 kN/m at B).  
   Tasks:
   - Determine reactions
   - Draw SF & BM diagrams
   - Find location of maximum BM
