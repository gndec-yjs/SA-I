## 📚 Course Content

### **Part A**

**[Unit 1](Unit_1.md):**
Load – types and their assessment; Types of supports; Concept and types of structure – cables, trusses, beams, arches, frames; Statical determinacy; Identification of determinate and indeterminate structures based on Degree of Redundancy (DoR) **(3L)**

📌 **[Assignment 1](Assignments/Assignment_1.md)**

**[Unit 2](Unit_2.md):**
Internal forces in determinate structures – axial force, shear force, bending moment, sign conventions; SFD & BMD for cantilever, simply supported and overhang beams subjected to various loads; Point of contra-flexure; Displacements (deflection & rotation); Direct integration, Moment area, Conjugate beam & Energy methods **(18L)**

📌 **[Assignment 2](Assignments/Assignment_2.md)**

### **Part B**

**[Unit 3](Unit_3.md):**
Effect of load on indeterminate structures – displacements & internal forces; Analysis of arches, beams & frames having **DoR ≤ 2**; Method of Consistent Deformation; Three-Moment Theorem **(12L)**

📌 **[Assignment 3](Assignments/Assignment_3.md)**

**[Unit 4](Unit_4.md):**
Influence Line Diagrams (ILD); Moving loads; Bending moment & shear force diagrams due to single/multiple concentrated rolling loads & UDL; Equivalent UDL; Muller-Breslau Principle; ILD for beams, trusses & portal frames; Maximum & absolute maximum effect; Envelopes **(12L)**

📌 **[Assignment 4](Assignments/Assignment_4.md)**
