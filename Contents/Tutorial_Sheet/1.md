# **TUTORIAL SHEET – 1** 

## **Course:** Structural Analysis - I
## **Topic:** Shear Force & Bending Moment Diagrams (SFD & BMD)  

### **Learning Objectives**
After completion, a student should be able to:
- Understand concepts of **Shear Force (SF)** & **Bending Moment (BM)**
- Draw **SF & BM Diagrams** for basic beams
- Handle common loading patterns: **Point Load**, **UDL**, **UVL**, **Moment**
- Identify **Point of Contraflexure (POC)**

### **Problem-2: Eccentric Point Load on Simply Supported Beam**

*Image: SSB with eccentric point load at distance 'a' from left*

**Tasks:**
- Determine reactions
- Derive $V(x)$ and $M(x)$ for regions:

- Plot SFD & BMD
- Find location and value of maximum BM
