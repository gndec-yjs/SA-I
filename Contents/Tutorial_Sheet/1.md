## Learning Objectives

After completing this tutorial, students will be able to:

- Understand concepts of **Shear Force (SF)** and **Bending Moment (BM)**.
- Draw SF and BM diagrams for common beam systems.
- Analyse beams under **point load, UDL, UVL and moment loading**.
- Identify and interpret **Point of Contraflexure (POC)**.
