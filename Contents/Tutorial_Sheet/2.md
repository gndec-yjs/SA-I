## Learning Objectives

After completing this tutorial, students will be able to:

- Differentiate between **statically determinate** and **statically indeterminate** structures.
- Define **Degree of Redundancy / Static Indeterminacy**.
- Compute indeterminacy for beams, trusses, and frames.
- Classify structures as determinate, indeterminate, or unstable.
