## Learning Objectives

After completing this tutorial, students will be able to:

- Apply **energy principles** in structural analysis  
- Compute **deflections using any suitable energy method (strain energy and unit load methods)**  
- Apply **Castigliano’s theorem** for displacement calculation  
- Analyse **beams and frames using energy approaches**
