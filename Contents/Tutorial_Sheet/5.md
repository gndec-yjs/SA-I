## Learning Objectives

After completing this tutorial, students will be able to:

- Understand the **behavior of cables under different loading conditions**
- Analyse **cables subjected to concentrated loads and UDL**
- Determine **tension, reactions, and cable profile**
- Apply concepts to **suspension bridges**
- Evaluate **horizontal thrust and sag relationships**
